# TLOS Intelligence Reports

## Purpose

The **Reports** layer contains structured outputs generated from one or more intelligence engines.

Reports convert analytical intelligence into decision-ready documents for founders, business leaders, and future AI workflows.

Reports should present synthesized insights rather than raw data.

---

# Design Principles

Every report should:

- Be evidence-based.
- Clearly distinguish observations from recommendations.
- Reference the intelligence engines used.
- Maintain a consistent structure.
- Support executive decision-making.
- Be reusable and version controlled.

Reports may be referenced by:

- Automation

- Executive decision-making

# Guiding Principle

Reports communicate intelligence.

They should transform analytical outputs into clear, actionable, and evidence-based insights.
