# Product Principles

These design principles govern the development, evolution, and usage of the Thought Leadership Operating System (TLOS).

---

## Principle 1: Signal Density Above All Else
Every document produced by TLOS must maximize original insight per word. Fluff, generic AI preamble, and buzzword padding are explicitly disallowed.

## Principle 2: Human Intent, Machine Acceleration
The human founder provides the vision, core thesis, edge-case insight, and nuance. The AI system handles structural synthesis, stylistic formatting, expansion, and multi-channel adaptation.

## Principle 3: Markdown-First & Git-Native
All knowledge artifacts, prompt skills, and templates are stored as version-controlled Markdown (`.md`) files. This ensures portability, zero vendor lock-in, and full auditability over time.

## Principle 4: Modular Skill Primitives
Skills are isolated, reproducible execution units. A skill takes structured inputs from `/knowledge` and `/research`, processes them through targeted prompt recipes, and outputs standardized deliverables.

## Principle 5: Deterministic Quality & Human Auditing
No artifact is published automatically without passing through a human-in-the-loop review stage. High-value communications demand verified authority.
