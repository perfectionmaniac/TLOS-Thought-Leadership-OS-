# Founder Operating Guide: Executing TLOS

> *A practical playbook for executives to build institutional authority using TLOS.*

---

## 1. Daily & Weekly Cadence

### Daily Signal Capture (10 minutes/day)
- Dump raw voice transcripts, meeting notes, slack thoughts, or architectural breakthroughs into `/knowledge`.
- Do not worry about formatting, grammar, or structure. Raw signal is what matters.

### Weekly Synthesis (1 hour/week)
1. **Review & Tag**: Clean up new entries in `/knowledge`.
2. **Select Skill**: Choose a production target from `/skills` (e.g., `technical_essay_skill.md`).
3. **Execute Generation**: Run the skill against selected `/knowledge` and `/research` items.
4. **Human Review**: Polish the generated draft to enforce voice consistency.

---

## 2. Converting Ideas into Impact

```
Raw Thought ──► /knowledge/ ──► Executed /skills/ ──► Staged in /templates/ ──► Published
```

---

## 3. Checklist for High-Signal Thought Leadership

- [ ] Does this piece present a unique counter-intuitive perspective?
- [ ] Is it grounded in real technical or operational experience?
- [ ] Have generic LLM intro/outro sections been removed?
- [ ] Is the publication channel aligned with the target audience?
