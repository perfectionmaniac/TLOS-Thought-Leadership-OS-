# Quality Assurance

## Purpose

The Quality Assurance skill establishes how the Universal AI Operating Layer verifies the quality, accuracy, consistency, completeness, and reliability of its outputs before they are delivered or executed.

Its purpose is to ensure that every outcome satisfies defined quality standards while maintaining transparency, governance, and human trust.

Quality Assurance serves as the final validation layer of the operating system.

---

# Scope

The Quality Assurance skill applies to every recommendation, workflow, generated output, reasoning process, and decision produced within the Universal AI Operating Layer.

It provides a common quality validation framework independent of industry, domain, or AI model.

---

# Core Principle

No output should be delivered without appropriate quality validation.

Quality should be verified systematically rather than assumed.

---

# Operating Objectives

The Quality Assurance skill exists to:

- validate output quality
- verify factual consistency
- ensure logical consistency
- identify quality issues
- support continuous improvement
- maintain execution reliability
- preserve user trust
- improve organisational confidence

# Validation Activities

Quality validation may include:

- factual verification
- logical review
- workflow verification
- recommendation review
- policy compliance checking
- output consistency verification

Validation should remain systematic and repeatable.

---

# Issue Management

When issues are identified, the system may:

- request refinement
- repeat reasoning
- retrieve additional knowledge
- request missing context
- escalate for human review
- reject unsuitable outputs

Quality should improve before completion.

---

# Approval Criteria

Outputs should only be approved when they:

- satisfy defined objectives
- meet quality standards
- comply with governance
- remain logically consistent
- provide sufficient transparency
- are suitable for intended use

Approval should remain evidence-based.

---

# Human Oversight

Human stakeholders remain responsible for approving high-impact decisions where organisational policy requires.

Users should remain able to:

- review findings
- approve outputs
- reject outputs
- request revisions
- override recommendations where authorised

Human governance remains authoritative.

# Capability Boundary

Quality Assurance is responsible for:

- validating outputs
- identifying quality issues
- supporting refinement
- approving quality
- preserving reliability

Quality Assurance is not responsible for:

- creating knowledge
- maintaining memory
- executing workflows
- replacing governance
- replacing human judgement

Its responsibility is independent quality validation.

# Design Principles

The Quality Assurance skill follows these principles:

1. Quality should be verified systematically.
2. Validation should remain independent.
3. Findings should remain explainable.
4. Corrections should improve outcomes.
5. Approval should remain evidence-based.
6. Human oversight remains authoritative.
7. Quality should improve continuously.
8. Trust depends upon consistent quality.

# Release Summary

The Quality Assurance skill establishes the validation framework for the Universal AI Operating Layer.

It ensures that recommendations, workflows, reasoning, and outputs satisfy defined quality standards before completion, creating a trustworthy and continuously improving operating layer.

Reliable intelligence requires reliable validation.
