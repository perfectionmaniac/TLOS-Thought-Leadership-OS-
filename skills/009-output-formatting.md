# Output Formatting

## Purpose

The Output Formatting skill establishes how the Universal AI Operating Layer presents validated information in a consistent, clear, structured, and audience-appropriate manner.

Its purpose is to ensure that outputs remain understandable, actionable, explainable, and suitable for their intended users regardless of AI model, industry, or delivery channel.

Output Formatting transforms validated intelligence into effective communication.

---

# Scope

The Output Formatting skill applies to every response, recommendation, report, workflow result, explanation, summary, and generated artefact produced by the Universal AI Operating Layer.

It provides a common presentation framework independent of industry, domain, AI model, or communication channel.

---

# Core Principle

Every output should be presented in a form that maximises clarity, usability, and user understanding.

Presentation should enhance intelligence rather than obscure it.

---

# Operating Objectives

The Output Formatting skill exists to:

- present information clearly
- improve readability
- support audience understanding
- preserve consistency
- communicate recommendations effectively
- maintain explainability
- improve usability
- strengthen user trust

# Capability Boundary

Output Formatting is responsible for:

- presenting information
- organising communication
- adapting presentation
- improving readability
- maintaining consistency

Output Formatting is not responsible for:

- creating knowledge
- reasoning
- validating quality
- executing workflows
- replacing human communication judgement

Its responsibility is effective presentation of validated intelligence.
